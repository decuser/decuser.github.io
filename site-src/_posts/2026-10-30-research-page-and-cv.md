## A home for the research.

I've added a [Research](/research/) page to the site. It brings my academic publications together in one place, with direct links to the papers and a downloadable [curriculum vitae](/assets/pdf/scholar/Curriculum-Vitae.pdf).

<!--more-->

The publications are grouped into two areas: **Foundations**, where my recent work addresses information science, apprehension, and AI-mediated systems; and **Empirical Research**, which includes studies of information systems, human-computer interaction, healthcare, and technology use. Entries run in reverse chronological order within each section.

The blog isn't changing direction. I'll still write about the systems, languages, and contraptions I build and explore. The new page just makes the connection to the scholarly work easier to find, particularly where theory and implementation meet.
